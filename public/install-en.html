Install Picas y Fijas on your phone

No app store needed: the game is added to your home screen straight from the browser, in under ten seconds. It opens full screen, without the browser bar, and takes you back to your games in a single tap.

What you get

  • An icon on your home screen, like any other app.
  • Full screen: no address bar, more room for the board.
  • Turn alerts: on iPhone they only arrive once the game is installed.

On iPhone and iPad, with Safari

  1. 1Tap the Share button in the Safari bar.
  2. 2Scroll down and pick Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add, at the top right.
Turn alerts on iPhone only work inside the installed game: Safari does not allow notifications for an ordinary tab. If you want to know when it is your move without watching the screen, that is the reason to add it.

On Android, with Chrome

  1. 1Open the browser ⋮ menu, at the top right.
  2. 2Pick Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. 3Confirm with Install.
Shortcut: on Android, with the game open, an Install the app card usually shows up in the lobby. One tap and you are done, with nothing to hunt for in the menu.
Opened it from Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp? Those in-app browsers cannot install anything. Open picasyfijas.fans in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and try again.
